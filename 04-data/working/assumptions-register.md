# Assumptions Register

## Purpose
Track the assumptions behind the calculators so the product stays transparent and easier to maintain.

## Global assumptions
### Security deposit
- default planning assumption: 5% of annual rent for unfurnished
- optional future logic: 10% for furnished where applicable

### Agent fee
- default planning assumption: 5% of annual rent
- optional minimum-fee logic can be added later

### Registration fee
- Dubai planning assumption: around AED 220
- Abu Dhabi planning assumption: area-specific placeholder band until finalized

### Municipality fee
- Dubai planning method: annual rent * 0.05 / 12
- Abu Dhabi: keep local assumption explicit rather than copying Dubai logic blindly

### Utility deposit
- Dubai starter assumption: around AED 2000
- Abu Dhabi starter assumption: AED 1500 to 2500 band depending on property / setup

### Internet setup
- starter assumption: around AED 200 setup
- monthly internet estimate should remain a band, not a fake exact number

### Moving cost
- small apartment band: AED 1000 to 1800
- medium apartment band: AED 1500 to 2800
- larger move: higher custom estimate

## Communication rule
The public product should say:
- estimates are for planning
- exact charges vary by building, landlord, provider, and current rules
- assumptions are shown beside the result
