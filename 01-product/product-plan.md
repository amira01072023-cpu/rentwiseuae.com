# Product Plan

## Goal
Help UAE renters answer:
- Can I afford this apartment?
- How much cash do I need before moving in?
- What hidden costs will I pay?
- Is this area worth it once commute and lifestyle are included?
- Is the rent increase fair or likely legal?

## Unique product angle
This is **not** a listings site.
This is a **decision-support utility site**.

## Core differentiators
### 1. True Move-In Cost Calculator
Inputs:
- emirate
- area
- annual rent
- cheque count
- furnished / unfurnished
- AC / chiller included or not
- agent involved or direct
- property type

Outputs:
- security deposit estimate
- agent fee estimate
- Ejari / registration estimate
- DEWA / utility setup estimate
- municipality fee estimate
- internet setup estimate
- moving estimate
- total cash needed up front

### 2. True Monthly Cost Calculator
Outputs a memorable number:
**True Monthly Cost = AED X**

Includes:
- rent equivalent per month
- utilities
- cooling
- internet
- municipality fees
- parking estimate
- commute estimate

### 3. Salary-to-Rent Safety Calculator
Outputs:
- safe rent range
- stretch rent range
- risky rent range
- suggested areas

### 4. Commute + Rent Tradeoff Tool
Compares:
- cheaper home / longer commute
- pricier home / shorter commute

Calculates:
- annual cost difference
- monthly cost difference
- time lost per month

### 5. Hidden Cost Checklist Generator
Generates a practical checklist by renter type.

### 6. Rent Increase / Fairness Checker
Gives a practical guidance result, not legal advice.

## UX rules
- calculator-first homepage
- mobile-first
- fast results
- clear assumptions
- no clutter before value
- allow easy comparison and saving later

## Trust rules
- show last updated dates
- explain assumptions
- clearly separate estimate vs official fee
- keep language plain and practical

## What not to become
- generic blog
- property listings clone
- luxury lifestyle magazine
- SEO fluff site
