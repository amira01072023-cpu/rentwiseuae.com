# Branding Notes

## Project name
**RentWise UAE**

## Domain
`rentwiseuae.com`

## Positioning
A UAE rent utility site that helps people understand the true cost of renting before they sign.

## Brand direction
- practical
- trustworthy
- local
- clear, not flashy

## Homepage line
Calculate the true cost of renting in the UAE.

## Supporting line
Compare rent, move-in cash, utilities, and hidden costs before you sign.
